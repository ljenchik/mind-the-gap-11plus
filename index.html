How it works

1. Initial assessment

Two short tests, A and B, show which topics your child knows and which need more work.

2. Practise by topic

Over 6000 questions in 8 topics, from Number to Problem solving. Pick one topic or several.

3. 60 CSSE-style papers

Exam-like practice papers with the topics in the same order as the CSSE test. Paper 1 is the gentlest; each one after it is a little harder.

4. Fix my mistakes

New questions on the parts your child got wrong, until the method sticks.

How the papers open

Paper 1 is open from the start. The next paper opens when your child either:

  • scores 55% or more on their first try, or
  • finishes "Fix my mistakes" for that paper.

Only the first try counts towards their score. They can take a paper again, but a retake has different questions.
