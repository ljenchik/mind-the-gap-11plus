Last updated: [date]. Who we are: Mind the Gap 11+, run by [your name or company], [address]. Contact: [email]. We are the data controller.

For children (the short version)

This app helps you practise maths. It saves your answers so you can see how you are doing. We do not show you adverts, we do not let you chat with anyone, and we never sell anything about you. If something worries you, tell a grown-up.

What we keep, and why

  • Practice results (answers, scores, time spent, the made-up username and avatar your child picks). These are kept on your device so the app can show progress. If a parent signs in, they are also saved to the parent's account so they appear on other devices. Lawful basis: to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Children's photos chosen as an avatar stay on the device and are never uploaded.
  • Parent's email address, only if a parent creates an account, to send sign-in links. Lawful basis: contract.
  • Payment is handled by Stripe. We never see card details; we keep only the plan and when it ends. Lawful basis: contract and legal duties for tax records.
  • Anonymous question counts: how often each question is answered, missed or reported, with no link to any child or device. We use these to fix unclear questions. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (improving the questions).

What we do not do

  • No adverts, no advertising or tracking cookies, no analytics that follow your child.
  • No profiling for marketing, no selling or sharing data.
  • No chat, no contact with other users, no location.
  • Children do not create accounts and are never asked for their real name, email or school.

We follow the ICO's Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children's Code): high privacy by default, the least data we can, and no nudges to give more.

Who else handles data

  • Netlify hosts the website.
  • Supabase stores parent accounts and saved progress ([region, e.g. London / EU]).
  • Stripe takes payments.

Each acts on our instructions under a data processing agreement. Where data leaves the UK, it is protected by UK-approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

Saved progress: until you delete it or 12 months after the account was last used. Payment records: 6 years (tax law). Data on your device stays until you clear it in the app (Parents area) or in your browser.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your family's data, and object to how we use it. In the app, Parents → Plan and access → Delete my saved data removes everything saved in your account straight away. For anything else, email [email]; we reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell signed-in parents by email.
