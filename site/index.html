How it works

1. Initial assessment

A short check across all 8 topics gives a starting score and a first list of gaps.

2. Practise the gaps

Over 5,000 questions in 34 parts of the CSSE maths paper. Pick one topic or combine several.

3. Practice papers

Full 60-mark papers in the real CSSE order. The first try is the score that counts.

4. Fix my mistakes

After a paper, your child gets new questions on the parts they missed, not the same ones again, so there is nothing to copy.
